## Save GIF, directly from your offline animation

TypeBit 0.3.0 adds **Save GIF** to the HTML player. Choose a playback speed, export the full animation, and watch the progress. You can cancel; the player returns to its prior position and state. Encoding happens locally inside the downloaded HTML.

Three new original examples show the workflow in practice:

- **Leaf Studio:** a botanical brand introduction.
- **Coffee Story:** a cup, steam and beans for product storytelling.
- **White Letter Badge:** a transparent raster badge that keeps its white letters.

Explore the [case gallery](https://github.com/JulyanXu/typebit/blob/main/docs/cases/README.md), or download **typebit-cases-0.3.0.zip** below for the HTMLs, GIFs, PNGs and editable projects. Install **typebit-line-animation-0.3.0.zip** to use the updated Skill.

Browser GIF export uses white, up to 480 px width and 10 fps, with a 0.8-second final hold. Use HTML or PNG for transparency; the Agent/CLI supports more GIF size, frame-rate and background choices. No runtime CDN or new account is required.

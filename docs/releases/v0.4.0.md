## TypeBit is now TraceGIF

**Draw the lines. Make the GIF. Keep the source.** TraceGIF makes the project's two main uses explicit: line-drawing animation and GIF creation.

Install **tracegif-0.4.0.zip** and invoke `$tracegif` in Codex or `/tracegif` in Claude Code. Existing editable projects keep their artwork and can be rendered with the new player. See the [migration guide](https://github.com/JulyanXu/tracegif/blob/main/docs/migration.md) for legacy entrypoints.

### More control over GIF delivery

The offline HTML now has **GIF settings** for width, frame rate and solid background color. It previews the output dimensions and duration, gives feedback when settings exceed export limits, and keeps those preferences when saving HTML. Progress, cancellation and playback-state restoration remain available.

Resized GIF exports preserve exact white, black and matte colors; the toolbar remains usable on short screens. GIF uses a solid matte. Use HTML or PNG for smooth transparency.

Delivery checks now reject stale or altered exports and protect unrelated ZIPs and private revision snapshots. Packaging replaces its output atomically. Empty or fully opaque raster masks remain incomplete, and verification retries keep prior screenshots with their original report.

### Seven original scenarios

The [case gallery](https://github.com/JulyanXu/tracegif/blob/main/docs/cases/README.md) now includes four new examples: **Courtyard House**, **City Route**, **Orbit Lesson**, and **Celebration Card**, alongside the botanical identity, coffee illustration and white-letter badge.

Download **tracegif-cases-0.4.0.zip** for seven HTMLs, GIFs, transparent PNGs, editable projects and browser evidence. Every preview comes from the actual player. Landscape and portrait proportions are retained. The route is fictional, the architecture illustrative, and the orbital diagram not to scale.
